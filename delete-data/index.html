Your data, your call

Delete your Pawpon data

How to ask Ludilume, the developer of Pawpon, to delete the data connected to you, and exactly what happens when you do. You do not need an account and it costs nothing.

Pawpon for Android (com.pawpon) · Developer: Ludilume (Talha Abid) · Updated 24 September 2026

Ask us to delete your data

  1. Write to usSend an email to talhaabidj1@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Pawpon data. Send it from any address you like.
  2. Help us find your recordPawpon has no accounts, so the data we hold is filed under a random ID. If you ever bought something, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in the receipt Google emailed you). If you never bought anything, just tell us your phone model.
  3. We delete it and tell youWe delete the matching records within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm. We never ask for a password, and we will not ask you to prove anything beyond what is needed to find the right record.

Email a deletion request

Delete game data yourself, right now

Your progress is stored on your phone, not with us, so you can remove it without asking anyone:

  • In the game: open Settings, then Account, then Reset player data. This clears your collection, currencies and progress for good.
  • On your phone: uninstall Pawpon, or go to Android Settings, then Apps, then Pawpon, then Storage, and choose Clear storage.
  • Your Google backup: if Android backed up Pawpon to your Google account, you can remove that backup in your Google Drive or Google One backup settings.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhere it livesWhat happens
Game progress: cats, currencies, settingsYour phone onlyDeleted when you reset it in the game, clear the app's storage or uninstall. We never had a copy.
Purchase record: random app user ID, products bought, order IDs, device detailsRevenueCat, our purchase providerDeleted within 30 days of your request.
Emails you sent usOur inboxDeleted with your request, except the request itself, which we keep for up to 12 months as a record that we carried it out.
Google Play order historyGoogleKept by Google as a financial record under Google's own rules. We cannot delete it. You can see it in your Google Play account.
Play Games profile, scores and achievementsGoogleHeld by Google, not us. Delete it in the Play Games app (Settings, then Delete Play Games account and data) or in your Google Account.
Advertising data, in versions with adsGoogle AdMobHeld by Google. Delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).

Before you ask. Deleting your purchase record means we can no longer see what you bought. If you later tap Restore purchases, Google Play can still tell the game about the Nightlight Pass, because Google remains the record of what you own. Packs of gems, dust and coins that were already delivered are part of your save file, so they go when your save goes.

Deleting only some of it

You can ask for part of your data to be deleted, for example your old emails but not your purchase record. Say which in your message and we will do exactly that.
