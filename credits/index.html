Pawpon

Design, programming, art direction, sound and writing by Talha Abid at Ludilume. Everything you see and hear inside the game, including the cabinets, the cats and every whirr of the claw, is generated by code written for Pawpon.

Thank you to everyone who joined the closed test, sent a bug report or told us, bluntly, what did not feel right. The game is better for every one of you.
