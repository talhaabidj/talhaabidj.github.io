Every turn brings someone home.

Take the night shift at a little capsule shop on Lantern Row. Put a token in, take the joystick, and watch the claw carry a capsule to the chute. Then crack it open and see who came home. There are 36 cats in the machines, and every machine tells you its real odds before you spend a thing.

The ritual

Three small motions. One small cat.

Pawpon is played in first person, with your hands on the machine. It is a slow game on purpose. The best part of a capsule is the second before it opens.

  1. Drop a token

    A token goes in the slot. You earn tokens by working the shift, from the daily free pull and by finishing sets. They are never sold, not at any price.

  2. Work the claw

    Your hands take the joystick and the claw glides over the pile. Hit GRAB, watch it close on a capsule and lift, then DROP it down the chute.

  3. Crack it open

    The capsule rolls into the chute and you pop the shell. A new cat goes on your shelf. A duplicate turns into Moon Dust, so nothing is wasted.

    The collection

    42 cats, seven sets, a few secrets

    Six cabinets hold six cats each, from a frosting kitten that smells of vanilla to things we would rather you met in person. The six Afterglow companions come with the Nightlight Pass and are never put in a machine.

      Fair by design

      The odds are printed on the glass

      Capsule machines are supposed to be a little thrilling. They are not supposed to be a trap. So we made a few rules and wrote them into the game.

      • Nothing sold is randomRandom pulls cost tokens, and tokens can only be earned. Everything in the store has a known result that you see before you pay.
      • Real odds, before you spendEvery cabinet has an odds sheet worked out for your luck right now. The starting numbers are on this website too.
      • Every pull gets you closerA pity ladder guarantees each rarity within a set number of pulls, and the Lucky Meter shows you how close you are.
      • A dropped capsule costs nothingHeavy capsules sometimes slip from the claw. The retry is free, and try number 3 always lands.

      On the night shift

      There is more to the shop than the machines

      Night jobs

      Clear a jammed mechanism, wipe fingerprints off the glass, pick up after the customers. A finished shift pays your wages and covers the bills.

      Grow the shop

      Open new cabinets and upgrade them with Star Gems. Each level tips the odds toward the rarer cats, and the prize card shows exactly how far before you commit.

      Shelves at home

      Every cat you bring home can sit on a shelf in your bedroom. Arrange them by set, by colour or by who you like best. Nobody is judging.

      The Hush

      A quiet back room opens after a few nights. There is no roll in there at all: you choose the exact legendary or mythical cat you want and it comes home.

      Notes from M.

      The last keeper left in a hurry, mid-sentence. Their notes are taped to the machines and tucked into the sets, and a full shelf earns you a postcard.

      Offline and unhurried

      No account to make and no internet needed to play. Your progress is saved on your phone, and the shop keeps your place whenever you put it down.
